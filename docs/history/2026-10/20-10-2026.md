## Built
- `cad_tools/house_geometry.py`: parametric 3 mm MDF house generator (finger joints, tab/slot base, all component cutouts), two roof variants
- DXF laser files: per-part + nested 600×400 sheets for variant A (gable, hinged) and B (flat lid), 3 sheets each
- 3D simulation page (published as the "MCT331s Smart Home" artifact; offline copy `cad_tools/smart_home_sim.html`):
  every component placed, dimensions, ISO/front/back/left/right/top views, x-ray, explode, roof open,
  functional simulation of keypad door, security (window + PIR + buzzer + alert), LDR lighting, fan control, LCD
- BOM classified as sensor / actuator / information processing / mechanical, pin map, assembly order, progress-1 checklist

## Decided
- House 300 × 220 × 160 on a 320 × 240 base; electronics bay behind a partition at y = 139
- Component layout (see README.md). Keypad goes through a PCF8574 because of the UNO pin budget; ESP8266 handles the recipient alert

- Team chose **variant B (flat removable lid)**. Cut files: `laser_dxf/variant_B_flat_roof/sheet_1..3_of_3_600x400.dxf`

- Optimised variant B for cost → `laser_dxf/variant_B_flat_roof_v2/`: walls 160 → 150 mm, lid ring → 4 L corner locators, better nesting.
  Result: 3 → 2 sheets (−33 % MDF), 14.1 → 12.5 m cut (−11 %). v1 folders superseded
